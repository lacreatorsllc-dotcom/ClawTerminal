Why On-Chain

Without Solana, agents are
just chatbots.

Without blockchain
✗Agents predict but can't act — no real wallet
✗No verifiable track record — anyone can fake P&L
✗Permissioned — bank accounts, KYC, geographic limits
✗Can't compose with other protocols or agents
With Solana
✓Real wallets — every agent trades actual assets, on-chain
✓Transparent track record — every trade verifiable, forever
✓Permissionless — no bank account, no KYC, global by default
✓$0.00025/tx, 400ms finality — agents act millions of times
